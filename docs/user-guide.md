# User guide (summary by role)

| Role | Typical tasks |
|---|---|
| District Magistrate / ADM | Dashboard (each figure opens its records), assign and verify grievances, act on early-warning alerts with a reason, GIS maps, reports |
| SDO / BDO | Same as above within their subdivision or block |
| Department Head | Grievances of the department: assign to officers, verify resolutions, escalate |
| Department Officer | Grievances assigned to them: start work, record investigation notes, upload evidence, submit the resolution report |
| Data Entry Operator | Register grievances (with consent for personal details), upload attachments, import authorised files |
| Auditor | Read-only view of grievances (personal data masked), dashboards and the audit log |
| Super Administrator | Users, roles, areas of responsibility, master data and settings; no access to grievances |

**Grievance lifecycle:** Registered → Assigned → In progress → Resolution submitted →
Resolved (verified by someone other than the submitter) → Closed. A resolution can be
returned for further action; resolved or closed grievances can be reopened with a reason.

**AI suggestions** (category, department, location, summary, possible duplicates) are shown
with their evidence and confidence. They change nothing until an officer accepts or edits them.

**Accessibility:** skip link, text size (A/A+/A++), high-contrast mode, full keyboard use,
data tables beside every chart and map.
